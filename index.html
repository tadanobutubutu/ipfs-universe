Observation scope This browser’s reachable neighborhood plus explicitly imported local Kubo observations; tracked at 2,048 and rendered at 1,024, never the entire IPFS network.

The center represents this browser’s Helia node. Bright green nodes are currently connected to the browser and have a line to the center. Violet nodes were discovered by browser routing but are not connected. Neutral slate nodes are open connections reported by the separate local Kubo daemon; they are not browser connections and have no line to this center. Distance from the center maps measured latency; unmeasured peers occupy the outer orbit, and angle groups the observed transport. Decorative background stars do not represent peers. The faint outer orbit marks the imported Kubo field and is not a connection. A relay edge appears only when the relay ID is present in the observed peer set; arbitrary peer-to-peer topology is never inferred. Use the peer explorer for the same information in text form.
